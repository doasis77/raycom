My Story

Growing up, I was always fascinated by how machines work. My journey began when I took apart a broken radio just to see what was inside. Years later, that same curiosity drove me into the world of software development.

One of the turning points in my life was building a Sign Language Translator using Flask and JavaScript. It wasn’t just about code — it was about creating something that mattered. Since then, I’ve worked on everything from emergency alert systems to canteen ordering platforms.

I believe that technology can change lives — and I’m here to build the future, one line of code at a time.
